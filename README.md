#Project Title

Enterprise Fraud Risk & Security Monitoring System


#Project Overview

An end-to-end fraud intelligence and risk monitoring solution designed using Python, SQL Server, and Tableau to simulate enterprise-level fraud analytics, operational risk monitoring, and compliance reporting workflows.

##Business Problem

Financial institutions continuously face increasing fraud exposure due to high transaction volumes, evolving fraud patterns, and operational monitoring limitations. Traditional reporting systems often struggle to proactively identify suspicious activities and prioritize operational fraud risks efficiently.




##Project Objective

The objective of this project was to develop an enterprise-style fraud intelligence framework capable of performing transaction analysis, fraud scoring, operational alert generation, SQL-based analytical reporting, and interactive dashboard visualization.
